Settle dialog directions

Three shadcn/Mira-aligned compositions using restrained product UI patterns.

01 Settle first · recommended

Settle “New session”?

It’ll leave your active list, but its conversation and Pi session files will stay available.

Makes the reversible, common action primary. Delete opens a separate destructive confirmation.
02 Explicit choices

What should happen to “New session”?

Choose whether to keep its history or remove it completely.

Best when both actions are common and users benefit from comparing consequences directly.
03 Compact action sheet

Close “New session”

You can keep the session for later or remove it permanently.

The smallest footprint and strongest mobile behavior, but less conventional for desktop dialogs.
